## 1. Hard Cut

*Basic narrative · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Choose the end point of A and the start point of B. 2 Remove the gap and butt them together. 3 Check eyeline, action and sound.
- **Use when:** Dialogue, documentary, information-dense content
- **Effect:** Clean and direct; keeps the narrative pace.
- **AI prompt:** `Hard cut from the end of A straight to B, keep the action continuous, no transition effect.`
- **Pitfalls:** Make sure the shot logic works first.
- **Sources:** S01 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

<a id="t2"></a>

## 2. Cut on Action

*Basic narrative · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Find a cut point mid-action. 2 Bring B in on the continuation of the same action. 3 Trim frame by frame to remove repeated or missing motion.
- **Use when:** Opening doors, turning, picking things up, sport
- **Effect:** Uses movement to lead the eye so the cut goes unnoticed.
- **AI prompt:** `Cut from A mid-way through raising the hand to B continuing the motion; match direction and speed.`
- **Pitfalls:** Action phase and screen axis must match.
- **Sources:** S02 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/match-cuts-creative-transitions-examples/)

<a id="t3"></a>

## 3. Graphic Match

*Match cuts · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Pick similar silhouettes. 2 Align position, scale and rotation. 3 Cut where the compositions are closest.
- **Use when:** Brand logos, round objects, cross-era storytelling
- **Effect:** Creates a visual link or metaphor.
- **AI prompt:** `Align the round subject in A with the round subject in B, match size, then hard cut.`
- **Pitfalls:** Keep the subject's position identical; avoid extra distortion.
- **Sources:** S02 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/match-cuts-creative-transitions-examples/)

<a id="t4"></a>

## 4. Color Match

*Match cuts · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Find shots with a similar dominant colour. 2 Correct colour temperature and brightness. 3 Cut to B when the same colour fills the frame.
- **Use when:** Fashion, food, travel, brand colours
- **Effect:** Keeps colour continuity across scenes.
- **AI prompt:** `Hard cut when A and B share the same dominant colour; unify brightness and colour temperature.`
- **Pitfalls:** Colour matching is not the same as applying one LUT to both shots.
- **Sources:** S02 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/match-cuts-creative-transitions-examples/)

<a id="t5"></a>

## 5. Motion Match

*Match cuts · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Find movement in the same direction. 2 Align speed and subject position. 3 Cut where the motion phases are closest.
- **Use when:** Throws and catches, rotating products, sports montages
- **Effect:** Joins different scenes into one continuous momentum.
- **AI prompt:** `A moving right cuts to B moving right; match speed and subject position, then cut.`
- **Pitfalls:** Can cross scenes; different from a continuity cut on the same action.
- **Sources:** S02 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/match-cuts-creative-transitions-examples/)

<a id="t6"></a>

## 6. Jump Cut

*Basic narrative · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Use footage from the same camera position. 2 Remove pauses or intervening time. 3 Keep the intentional jump in pose.
- **Use when:** Talking-head videos, tutorials, time compression
- **Effect:** Speeds things up and emphasises the passage of time.
- **AI prompt:** `Remove the pauses in A, keep same-angle jump cuts, and make the sentences flow naturally.`
- **Pitfalls:** Accept the visible jump; don't clip the ends of words.
- **Sources:** S01 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

<a id="t7"></a>

## 7. Smash / Contrast Cut

*Basic narrative · Editing AI · Instant*

- **How:** 1 Pick two segments with strong emotional or sonic contrast. 2 Hard cut on the reaction point. 3 Switch or cut the sound abruptly at the same moment.
- **Use when:** Comedic reversals, waking from a dream, sharp mood shifts
- **Effect:** Creates surprise, irony or impact.
- **AI prompt:** `Hard cut from the loudest moment of A to the silence of B; the sound stops at the same instant.`
- **Pitfalls:** The story should justify the contrast; don't do it just to show off.
- **Sources:** S01 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

<a id="t8"></a>

## 8. Cutaway

*Basic narrative · Editing AI · About 1–3 s for the insert*

- **How:** 1 Insert a relevant detail over the edit point in A. 2 Keep the original dialogue playing continuously. 3 Cut back to the main shot or into B.
- **Use when:** Removing lines from interviews, tutorials, documentary
- **Effect:** Hides the jump cut and adds information.
- **AI prompt:** `Insert related detail shot B over the removed line in A; keep the dialogue continuous.`
- **Pitfalls:** This is a shot-structure technique, not a software effect.
- **Sources:** S01 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

<a id="t9"></a>

## 9. J-cut

*Sound transitions · Editing AI · Audio leads by 0.3–1 s*

- **How:** 1 Split the audio and video edit points. 2 Let B's sound start before B's picture. 3 Balance levels across the handover.
- **Use when:** Interviews, arriving at a new location, suspenseful teasers
- **Effect:** Uses sound to set up the viewer's expectation.
- **AI prompt:** `Bring B's audio in 0.5 s early, then cut from A to B's picture.`
- **Pitfalls:** Requires an independently editable B audio track.
- **Sources:** S03 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/trim-clips/perform-j-cuts-and-l-cuts.html)

<a id="t10"></a>

## 10. L-cut

*Sound transitions · Editing AI · Audio trails by 0.5–2 s*

- **How:** 1 Cut the picture to B first. 2 Let A's dialogue or ambience continue. 3 Fade out A's audio at a natural sentence end.
- **Use when:** Interview reaction shots, voice-over over B-roll
- **Effect:** Preserves emotional and semantic continuity.
- **AI prompt:** `Cut to B's picture first; let A's dialogue continue to the end of the sentence, then fade out.`
- **Pitfalls:** Avoid A's audio masking B's dialogue.
- **Sources:** S03 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/trim-clips/perform-j-cuts-and-l-cuts.html)

<a id="t11"></a>

## 11. Sound Bridge

*Sound transitions · Editing AI · About 0.5–2 s across the cut*

- **How:** 1 Pick a sound that can carry across scenes. 2 Lay it over the picture cut. 3 Mix in B's ambience.
- **Use when:** Cross-location montages, flashbacks, thematic links
- **Effect:** Connects two spaces through sound.
- **AI prompt:** `Carry the same rain sound from A into B, gradually mixing in B's new ambience.`
- **Pitfalls:** Related to J/L-cuts but doesn't require dialogue.
- **Sources:** S02 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/match-cuts-creative-transitions-examples/)

<a id="t12"></a>

## 12. Cross Dissolve

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–1 s*

- **How:** 1 Keep extra handles on both clips. 2 Overlap A's tail with B's head. 3 Fade A's opacity down and B's up; check for ghosting.
- **Use when:** Passage of time, memories, calm landscapes
- **Effect:** Soft transition that implies a time relationship.
- **AI prompt:** `0.6 s cross dissolve from A to B with steady exposure.`
- **Pitfalls:** Two overlapping faces can produce messy ghosting.
- **Sources:** S07, S17 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/types-of-effects/transitions.html)

<a id="t13"></a>

## 13. Dip to Black

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.6–1.2 s*

- **How:** 1 Fade A down to black. 2 Optionally hold on black briefly. 3 Bring B up from black to normal exposure.
- **Use when:** End of a chapter, large time jumps, section breaks
- **Effect:** Creates a pause and a clear sense of sections.
- **AI prompt:** `Fade A to black over 0.3 s, then fade into B over 0.3 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Frequent dips to black can be mistaken for the ending.
- **Sources:** S07 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/types-of-effects/transitions.html)

<a id="t14"></a>

## 14. Flash / Dip to White

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.15–0.3 s*

- **How:** 1 Brighten A's tail. 2 Cut to B at peak white. 3 Bring B back to normal exposure quickly.
- **Use when:** Camera flashes, memory triggers, music beats
- **Effect:** Instant impact that masks the scene change.
- **AI prompt:** `Flash A to white then cut to B, return to normal exposure within 0.2 s, flash only once.`
- **Pitfalls:** Avoid repeated full-screen flashing.
- **Sources:** S07 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/types-of-effects/transitions.html)

## 15. Luma Fade

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Stack A and B. 2 Use A's luminance as a matte. 3 Animate the threshold so B appears through light and dark areas in turn.
- **Use when:** Skies, mountains, high-contrast silhouettes
- **Effect:** Reveals in layers that follow the image structure; richer than a plain dissolve.
- **AI prompt:** `Reveal B through A from highlights to shadows; 0.6 s luma fade.`
- **Pitfalls:** Low-contrast footage needs threshold and edge softness adjusted.
- **Sources:** S06, S05 · [main source](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/hub/features/why-use-a-luma-fade.html)

<a id="t16"></a>

## 16. Light Leak

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.3–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Place light-leak footage above both clips. 2 Use Screen or Add blending. 3 Align the brightest point with the cut, then fade out.
- **Use when:** Weddings, travel, retro lifestyle
- **Effect:** Warm glow and film texture.
- **AI prompt:** `Overlay one warm light leak at the A-to-B cut, peak covering the cut, 0.5 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** The leak should feel like a change in light and must not cover key information.
- **Sources:** S11, S27 · [main source](https://tutvid.com/premiere-pro/create-light-leak-transition-premiere-pro/)

<a id="t17"></a>

## 17. Film Burn

*Fades & light · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Add a burn texture or procedural effect. 2 Expand bright edges and an irregular matte. 3 Burn through A to reveal B.
- **Use when:** Retro music videos, flashbacks, film-look ads
- **Effect:** Organic, gritty material change.
- **AI prompt:** `A is overtaken by a warm film burn from the edges, revealing B after 0.5 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Unlike a uniform flash, it needs an irregular texture.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t18"></a>

## 18. Whip Pan

*In-camera seamless · Editing AI / Generative video AI · 0.2–0.5 s total whip*

- **How:** 1 End A with a fast whip to the right. 2 Start B whipping in from the same direction. 3 Cut at peak blur and add a short whoosh.
- **Use when:** Travel, sport, fast-paced B-roll
- **Effect:** Fast lateral momentum that hides the cut.
- **AI prompt:** `A whips right into B whipping in from the same direction; cut at peak motion blur.`
- **Pitfalls:** Same direction, similar speed and exposure; plan it at the shoot when possible.
- **Sources:** S12, S04 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/swish-pan-whip-pan-definition-film/)

<a id="t19"></a>

## 19. Occlusion Cut

*In-camera seamless · Editing AI / Generative video AI · About 0.1–0.3 s of occlusion*

- **How:** 1 Push A into a hand or dark object. 2 Wait until the frame is fully covered. 3 Cut to B emerging from the same occlusion.
- **Use when:** Outfit changes, space travel, fake one-takes
- **Effect:** The cut is hidden inside the occlusion.
- **AI prompt:** `Cut to B when A is fully covered by black clothing; B continues out from behind the same occlusion.`
- **Pitfalls:** Must truly fill the frame; match movement and occluder colour.
- **Sources:** S01, S04 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

## 20. Object Wipe

*Masking & compositing · Editing AI · 0.3–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Put B below A. 2 Track the edge of a foreground object passing through A. 3 Animate a mask along that edge to reveal B.
- **Use when:** Passing pillars, vehicles, pedestrians, door frames
- **Effect:** The scene looks naturally wiped away by a foreground object.
- **AI prompt:** `Track the pillar moving left to right in A and use its edge to wipe in B.`
- **Pitfalls:** Check mask edges and motion blur frame by frame.
- **Sources:** S10, S22 · [main source](https://www.adobe.com/learn/after-effects/web/create-custom-transitions)

<a id="t21"></a>

## 21. Subject Mask

*Masking & compositing · Editing AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Duplicate and cut out the subject. 2 Put the subject layer on top. 3 Change the background to B first, then hand over the subject.
- **Use when:** Personal promos, product ads, fashion
- **Effect:** The subject stays continuous while the space around it changes abruptly.
- **AI prompt:** `Keep A's subject in the foreground, wipe the background to B, then hand over naturally to B's subject.`
- **Pitfalls:** Hair, transparent objects and overlaps need edge cleanup.
- **Sources:** S22 · [main source](https://www.kylerholland.com/blog/object-mask-transitions-premiere-pro/)

<a id="t22"></a>

## 22. Zoom In

*Camera moves & speed · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.25–0.5 s*

- **How:** 1 Align both shots' anchor points on the same spot. 2 Rapidly scale up A's tail with radial blur. 3 Bring B back from the zoomed state.
- **Use when:** Product details, travel landmarks, beat-synced short videos
- **Effect:** Pushes forward into the next shot.
- **AI prompt:** `Quickly push in on A around the subject's centre, cut to B at peak blur, settle within 0.4 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Align the visual centre first; don't reveal frame edges.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t23"></a>

## 23. Zoom Out

*Camera moves & speed · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.25–0.5 s*

- **How:** 1 Rapidly scale down and blur A. 2 Bring B in at a larger scale. 3 Keep the pull-out direction consistent and return to normal framing.
- **Use when:** Detail to wide, spatial reveals, brand films
- **Effect:** Releases from the detail into a larger space.
- **AI prompt:** `Quickly pull out of A into B's wide shot, keep centre and direction consistent, 0.4 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** When shrinking A, fill the background or mirror-extend the edges.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t24"></a>

## 24. Spin Transition

*Camera moves & speed · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.3–0.6 s*

- **How:** 1 Accelerate A rotating around its centre. 2 Cut to B at peak blur. 3 Decelerate B in the same direction until it settles.
- **Use when:** Dance, sport, trend-driven ads
- **Effect:** Rotational energy and rhythmic impact.
- **AI prompt:** `A spins clockwise into B decelerating in the same direction, with motion blur, settling in 0.4 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Fill the frame edges; don't let the subject flip over for no reason.
- **Sources:** S07 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/types-of-effects/transitions.html)

<a id="t25"></a>

## 25. Speed Ramp

*Camera moves & speed · Editing AI · 0.4–1 s ramp*

- **How:** 1 Set time-remapping keyframes. 2 Speed up A's tail and bring B in at the same speed. 3 Slow B down to normal or slow motion.
- **Use when:** Sport, product B-roll, travel
- **Effect:** Highlights the peak of the action and lifts the pace.
- **AI prompt:** `Speed up the end of A, cut to B at peak speed, B smoothly slows to normal.`
- **Pitfalls:** Source frame rate limits slow-mo quality; the speed curve is not a fixed template.
- **Sources:** S08 · [main source](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/hub/guides/premiere-pro-speed-ramp.html)

<a id="t26"></a>

## 26. Defocus

*Blur & distortion · Editing AI / Generative video AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Rack A out of focus. 2 Bring B in on an out-of-focus head. 3 Rack B back into focus; lens blur can simulate it in post.
- **Use when:** Dreams, emotion, soft product reveals
- **Effect:** Hides the scene change inside visual blur.
- **AI prompt:** `A gradually defocuses to full blur, cut to B, then refocus; 0.6 s total.`
- **Pitfalls:** Match the colour blocks and brightness of both blurred frames.
- **Sources:** S01 · [main source](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-editing-transitions-in-film/)

<a id="t27"></a>

## 27. Linear Blur

*Blur & distortion · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.2–0.5 s*

- **How:** 1 Overlap A and B. 2 Ramp directional blur to a peak at the midpoint. 3 Dissolve to B and clear the blur.
- **Use when:** Tech, sport, motion graphics
- **Effect:** Directional sense of speed.
- **AI prompt:** `0.3 s horizontal blur dissolve from A to B, blurriest at the midpoint, sharp at both ends.`
- **Pitfalls:** Unlike a real whip pan, it works with static shots.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t28"></a>

## 28. Ripple

*Blur & distortion · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Animate a ripple displacement. 2 Mix in B at the ripple peak. 3 Reduce the ripple until B is stable.
- **Use when:** Dreams, memories, water themes, retro looks
- **Effect:** Space ripples like liquid.
- **AI prompt:** `A shows a gentle spreading ripple and transitions to B, stabilising within 0.6 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Large ripples distort faces.
- **Sources:** S13, S15 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

## 29. Morph Cut

*Blur & distortion · Editing AI · Start testing around 0.1–0.3 s*

- **How:** 1 Remove the pause. 2 Add a Morph Cut between two segments with similar poses. 3 After analysis, check mouth, eyes and background.
- **Use when:** Locked-off interviews, single-speaker talks
- **Effect:** Reduces the visual jump caused by removing lines.
- **AI prompt:** `Add a short Morph Cut to the same-angle talking-head jump cut; keep mouth shape and background stable.`
- **Pitfalls:** Not suitable for different people or large movements.
- **Sources:** S09 · [main source](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/apply-video-transitions/morph-cut-overview.html)

## 30. Glitch

*Stylised effects · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.1–0.3 s*

- **How:** 1 Add short RGB split and horizontal offset near the cut. 2 Cut to B in the middle. 3 Quickly return the effect to zero.
- **Use when:** Esports, digital products, malfunction storylines
- **Effect:** Digital interference and instability.
- **AI prompt:** `Add a 0.2 s RGB split and horizontal glitch from A to B, then return to clean immediately.`
- **Pitfalls:** Don't distort small text or brand logos for long.
- **Sources:** S15, S07 · [main source](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions)

## 31. Pixelize

*Stylised effects · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.3–0.6 s*

- **How:** 1 A's pixel blocks grow from fine to coarse. 2 Cut to B at the coarsest point. 3 B's blocks shrink back to sharp.
- **Use when:** Gaming, tech, retro pixel style
- **Effect:** Digital break-down and reassembly.
- **AI prompt:** `Pixelate A into large blocks, cut to B, then return to sharp; 0.5 s total.`
- **Pitfalls:** Not for frames where text must stay readable.
- **Sources:** S15 · [main source](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions)

## 32. Slide / Push

*Graphic & spatial · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.3–0.6 s*

- **How:** 1 Lay A and B side by side. 2 Move both left by one screen at the same speed. 3 Ease B into place.
- **Use when:** Tutorial steps, product features, info cards
- **Effect:** Clearly expresses sequence and spatial relationship.
- **AI prompt:** `A moves out to the left as B pushes in from the right in sync, easing in over 0.4 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** Unlike a wipe, both images move.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t33"></a>

## 33. Wipe

*Graphic & spatial · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.3–0.6 s*

- **How:** 1 Put B below A. 2 Sweep a straight-edge mask from left to right. 3 A is progressively replaced by B.
- **Use when:** Chapter changes, charts, brand animation
- **Effect:** Replaces the image cleanly and crisply.
- **AI prompt:** `Replace A with B using a left-to-right linear wipe, 0.4 s, crisp edge.`
- **Pitfalls:** The subject doesn't need to follow the wipe edge.
- **Sources:** S10, S13 · [main source](https://www.adobe.com/learn/after-effects/web/create-custom-transitions)

<a id="t34"></a>

## 34. Iris

*Graphic & spatial · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Create a circular mask. 2 Centre it on the focal point. 3 Grow the radius from zero to full frame.
- **Use when:** Playful, retro, spotlighting a detail, circular brand elements
- **Effect:** Focuses on the subject, then reveals the next scene.
- **AI prompt:** `Centred on B's subject, a circular mask grows from small to large to reveal B, 0.5 s.`
- **Pitfalls:** The centre must match the visual focal point.
- **Sources:** S13 · [main source](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/)

<a id="t35"></a>

## 35. Clock Wipe

*Graphic & spatial · Editing AI / Code AI · 0.4–0.8 s*

- **How:** 1 Create a sector mask. 2 Rotate it open around the centre. 3 B is fully revealed after one full turn.
- **Use when:** Timers, stage changes, graphic demos
- **Effect:** Implies time passing or a step completed.
- **AI prompt:** `Clockwise sector wipe from 12 o'clock reveals B, completing one turn in 0.6 s.`

## 38. First–Last-Frame Spatial Bridge

*Generative bridges · Generative video AI · Generate 2–5 s, then pick a section*

- **How:** 1 Take A's last frame and B's first frame. 2 Feed them to a model that supports first/last frames. 3 Describe a single motion path, then trim the seam after generation.
- **Use when:** Flying through doorways, architectural spaces, fantasy ads
- **Effect:** Generates continuous spatial motion that the original footage doesn't have.

## 39. Subject Morph

*Generative bridges · Generative video AI · Generate 2–5 s, then pick a section*

- **How:** 1 Choose A and B subjects with similar silhouettes. 2 Provide first and last reference frames. 3 Lock background and camera, then generate the transformation.
